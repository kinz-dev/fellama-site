譚仔新聞 · TCN

An experimental 24-hour Cantonese news station, drawn in pixel art and read by synthetic voices.

The station reads programmes prepared by its newsroom tools: morning and evening news, finance, technology, entertainment and travel. Its anchors are pixel-art characters; their voices and scripts are made with AI, and every broadcast says so.

Watch: the station streams on YouTube at youtube.com/@tamchainews.

TCN Live Scheduler

The station's own scheduling tool signs in to the TCN YouTube channel to create one live broadcast per programme, give it the programme's title, and switch between them as the day goes on. It only ever acts on the TCN channel, for the channel's owner. See the privacy policy.
